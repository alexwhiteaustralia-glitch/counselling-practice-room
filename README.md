# The Practice Room

A counselling skills coach for counselling and psychotherapy students. Claude teaches each skill, drills it with you, plays a simulated client in voice or text, and gives you observer feedback and a structured debrief afterwards.

Built by a counselling student who wanted more reps between triads. It draws on widely used frameworks: person-centred practice (Rogers), the microskills hierarchy (after Ivey), therapeutic presence (Geller), motivational interviewing (Miller and Rollnick), the stages of change model (Prochaska and DiClemente) and explanatory model questions (Kleinman). It isn't tied to any course or institution. If your training teaches something differently, follow your training.

## Training only

This is skills practice, not therapy, counselling or clinical supervision. The simulated clients never bring crisis or risk content. Don't bring real client material into it, apart from training sessions where recording was agreed (see Replay below). If practice stirs up something of your own, take it to your supervisor or a counsellor.

## What it does

- **Learn:** plain-language teaching for 21 skills across four levels, from opening and listening through to rupture and repair and ending well.
- **Drill:** short, targeted reps with a verdict and one reason each time: skill drills, warm-ups, ladder drills up the microskills hierarchy, change talk, culture, closes, repairs and more.
- **Practise:** full sessions with a simulated client you set up (gender, age, openness, style, depth, phase, theme, background), rehearsals for assessed sessions, and deep sessions that reward following what's underneath.
- **Edge drill:** reps built around your own recurring habits, like the tack-on question or reassuring when a client shrinks.
- **Replay:** paste the transcript of a real training session (triad, intensive, recorded assessment), get a short review, then re-run it from the moment that mattered and rewind to try again.
- **Casebook:** nine ongoing clients who remember you, warm up, withdraw and open up over a course of sessions, depending on how well you meet them.
- **Track:** a practice log, progress reports, and a favourites list of the lines you most want to own.

## How to use it

1. Install the plugin from the Claude directory.
2. Optional but recommended: in a Claude Project (or a folder Claude can read and write), add an empty file called `Practice_Log.md`. The room adds a short note after each session when it can save files.
3. Open a text chat and say **"practice room"**. Pick your settings and tap Start.
4. Switch to voice mode and say **"ready"**. Practise. Say **"end session"** when you're done, and the debrief follows.
5. Run your own timer. Claude can't keep time.

## Example prompts

- "practice room" (opens the setup card)
- "drill reflecting feeling"
- "rehearsal for a 20-minute assessed session next week"
- "edge drill reassurance"
- "replay" (then paste a training session transcript)
- "session with Greg" (a casebook client)
- "report"

## Privacy

What the plugin reads, writes and sends:


The plugin is instructions only. It runs no code, connects to no services, and sends nothing anywhere. It reads and writes only these files, in a folder or Project you choose, and only when you ask for something that needs them: `Practice_Log.md`, `Favourite_Skills.md`, `Casebook_Notes.md` and `Casebook_Sealed.md`. Session notes keep the simulated client's story to a one-line theme, and replays never store real names or transcripts.
